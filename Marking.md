# Hackathon Judging Criteria

**Total: 100 points — reviewed by at least 3 judges**

| Criterion | Points | What Judges Look For |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| **Impact** | 30 | How effectively the solution addresses a meaningful real-world problem for its intended users |
| **Technical** | 20 | How well the solution is built — implementation, reliability, and architecture |
| **Innovation** | 15 | How original or creative the approach is |
| **Demo** | 15 | How clearly the demo shows the solution, functionality, and value it provides |
| **Product & UX** | 10 | How intuitive, accessible, and easy to use the product is |
| **Sponsor Tech** | 10 | How effectively the solution uses sponsors' technology to enhance the product |
| **Total** | **100** | |

---

## Priority Order
1. 🥇 Impact (30pts) — solve a real problem well
2. 🥈 Technical (20pts) — build it properly
3. 🥉 Innovation (15pts) — be creative
4. Demo (15pts) — show it clearly
5. Product & UX (10pts) — make it usable
6. Sponsor Tech (10pts) — use their tools meaningfully
